To My Neo, My Ms Masengi ♡

My love,

Happy one month, Neo. 💜

It's crazy to think that it's already been a month of us. One month of little moments, conversations, laughter, and memories that have slowly become some of my favourite things to look back on. I don't know if a month sounds like a long time to anyone else, but to me, it's been enough time for you to become someone who means more to me than I sometimes know how to explain.

Sometimes I think about our first date, after watching Spider-Man. I think about how we ended up spending hours just talking, how one conversation turned into another, until eventually we started holding hands. It's such a simple memory, but there's something about it that makes me want to keep it close forever. If I could go back to that night, knowing how much you would come to mean to me, I'd choose to live it all over again. Every conversation, every little moment, every second that brought us closer. I'd want to experience it all again, because it led me to you.

You make me feel something that I don't think I can put into perfect words. You make me feel like I matter to someone, like I have a place in someone's heart. You make me feel fulfilled in a way I didn't know I needed, and there's a comfort in knowing that I have you in my life. It's not that the world suddenly becomes perfect when you're around. It's that having you makes the ordinary parts of it feel a little more beautiful.

And Neo, I really wish you knew how much you actually mean to me.

Sometimes I stay up late thinking about you, replaying little moments in my head, thinking about our conversations, or remembering your voice. Sometimes I catch myself smiling at the smallest things because somehow, they remind me of you. And even when I'm joking around, teasing you, or acting like I'm not being serious, there's a part of me that hopes you know there's so much love behind all of it.

Because, Neo, I really do love you. More than my jokes probably let on, more than I always know how to express, and more than these words could ever fully explain. I don't want you to have to guess how much you matter to me. I want you to feel it in the way I care about you, the way I listen to you, the way I remember the little things, and the way I keep choosing to be here with you.

I want to give this relationship my genuine effort, my patience, my honesty, and the best of what I have to offer. I want to be someone who makes you feel loved, valued, and appreciated, because you deserve to know that what we have matters to me.

One month ago, we were just beginning to write this little story of ours. Now, we have memories that belong only to us, moments I can look back on and smile about, and so many things we have yet to experience together. I know we still have so much to learn about each other, and I don't expect every day to be perfect. I just hope that, through the good days and the difficult ones, we keep choosing to understand each other, to communicate, and to grow together.

If there's one thing I want you to take from this letter, it's that you are not just someone I happen to have in my life. You are someone I am genuinely grateful for. Someone whose voice can stay in my thoughts long after we've said goodbye. Someone who can make an ordinary night feel unforgettable just by being there.

And if I could give you one thing, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just a moment. Maybe then you'd understand why I smile when I think about you, why that night after Spider-Man means so much to me, and why even the smallest memories with you have found such a special place in my heart.

Happy first month, Neo. ♡

Thank you for being part of my life, for the memories we've already made, and for giving me someone so special to look forward to. I don't know everything our future holds, but I know I'm excited to keep discovering it with you, one little moment at a time.

And if we ever look back on this letter months or years from now, I hope we smile at how our story began. I hope we remember that this was our first month, when we were still discovering all the little things about each other, and that even then, you already meant so much to me.

One month down, and so much more of our story left to write.

I love you, Neo. More than my jokes say, more than my shyness sometimes shows, and more than I could ever fit into one little letter.

Always your Fafankie, ♡
